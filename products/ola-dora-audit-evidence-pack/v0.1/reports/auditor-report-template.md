## Exceptions and limitations

Record SKIPPED exceptions, NOT_APPLICABLE rationale, unavailable source systems, declared reviewer identity/role and any manual evidence. Neither SKIPPED nor NOT_APPLICABLE is a PASS.

## Verification boundary

State what the pack recomputed and checked, what was manually supplied, what source identities were independently authenticated, and what remains unverified. A manifest digest is not a signature. The pack does not certify DORA compliance or guarantee auditor acceptance.
